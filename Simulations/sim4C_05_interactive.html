What you are looking at

An isothermal process holds temperature constant, so the gas moves along one hyperbola PV = nRT. Because the internal energy of an ideal gas depends only on T, ΔU = 0, so Q = W = nRT ln(V_f/V_i). When the gas expands (V_f > V_i) it does work on the piston, and an equal amount of heat must flow in from the reservoir. When the gas is compressed (V_f < V_i), work is done on the gas and the same amount of heat flows out to the reservoir. The shaded area under the curve is that work.
